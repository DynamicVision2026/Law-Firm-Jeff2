# North Star

**The free engine exists for one goal: make individuals want to share their case.**

Every feature, question, word and screen is judged by one test:
> Does this make a person more willing to **start**, **finish** and **share** their case?
If not, remove it or change it.

## Positioning: the legal pre-visit check

Like a symptom checker before seeing a doctor (e.g. Ubie in Japan: free for
people, institutions pay). The person knows where it hurts, not the legal
category. We sort it, show what's at stake and what help costs, and point to
the right next step. Lawyers = doctors, law firms = hospitals.
Not a diagnosis: general information only, never legal advice or a win %.

Two kinds of people come:
1. **"Someone owes me money and won't pay"** — can I get it back, how, is it worth the cost?
2. **"I don't know what I'm entitled to"** (traffic, divorce, insurance) — is the offer fair?

## Design rules that follow from the goal

1. **Lead with money.** The first thing people see in the result is a concrete
   estimate ("how much should you really get"), not "do you need a lawyer".
   Every market needs an amount table from its lawyers.
2. **Value before effort.** Easy questions first. Show the person that a useful
   answer is coming (e.g. "2 more questions to see your estimate").
3. **Feels like a quiz, not a legal form.** One question per screen, tap answers,
   progress bar, everyday words, always an "I don't know" option, ~2 minutes.
4. **Safe to share.** No names, addresses or contact details. Say so clearly
   at the start. Anonymous by default.
5. **Trust signals.** Show the real supervising law firm's name and 「弁護士監修」.
   Never promise results.
6. **Something to keep.** The result is a report the person can save or send
   (screenshot-friendly, LINE / WeChat share), so they come back and pass it on.
7. **Urgency is honest.** Deadlines and "don't sign yet" warnings are shown
   because they protect the person, and they also give a reason to act now.
8. **Start with the strongest case types.** Traffic accidents and unpaid
   wages / dismissal first (clear money). Divorce later.

## How we measure it (MVP)

| Stage | Measure | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Visitors who answer the first question | 50%+ |
| Finish | Starters who reach the result | 60%+ |
| Value | "Was this helpful?" yes | 70%+ |
| Share | Finishers who agree to anonymized sharing | 50%+ |
| Next step | Finishers who say they'd talk to a lawyer | track |

If these numbers are weak after 4–6 weeks live, change the design before
building the law-firm side.
